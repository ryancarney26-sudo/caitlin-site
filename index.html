Booking

  1. Send me the idea. A few lines, where on the body, rough size, and any reference pictures. Instagram messages or the studio's form both work.
  2. I come back with a quote. Usually within a few days. Bigger pieces are priced by the session.
  3. A deposit holds your slot. It comes off the final price.
  4. We tattoo. I draw the design up before the day, and we make any changes together before the stencil goes on.

Questions people ask

How much does a tattoo cost?

Small pieces have a minimum charge, and larger custom work is priced by the session. The prices page has the figures. Send me the idea and I'll give you a clear price before you pay anything.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes. Send a clear photo of the old tattoo in daylight and tell me what you'd like instead. Some old tattoos need a session of laser fading first, which the studio also offers. More on the cover-ups page.

Can I just walk in?

Often, yes, for small pieces. Ring the studio first on 01772 378565 to check I have a gap that day. Custom work is always booked in advance.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. The deposit holds your appointment and comes off the final price. It's non-refundable if you cancel at short notice.

How old do I need to be?

Eighteen, with photo ID on the day. That's the law in the UK, with no exceptions for parental consent.

How do I look after a new tattoo?

I'll go through aftercare with you at the end of your session and send you home with written instructions. In short: keep it clean, keep it moisturised with a thin layer, and keep it out of the sun and out of baths and pools for two weeks.

Can I bring a friend?

One friend is fine. The studio is a working space, so big groups aren't.
