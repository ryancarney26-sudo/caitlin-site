Memorial and family tattoos in Preston

A memorial tattoo carries someone with you. I make them at True Colours in Preston for people from across Lancashire: portraits, a loved one's handwriting, names and dates, children's names, and pieces for the family you have now.

How I work

[Caitlin, two or three lines: what memorial pieces mean to the people who come to you, and how you handle those sessions.]

Bring what you have. A photo, a birthday card in their handwriting, a date, a line from a song, the flower from the funeral. We build the design around the real things, not a stock image.

Handwriting can be tattooed exactly. A signature or a line from a card, photographed flat and sized to fit. I keep the wobble in. That's the point of it.

It doesn't have to be a portrait. A date in roman numerals, a pair of initials, a small bird, a single flower. The small pieces are often the ones people touch every day.

Family pieces. Children's names and birth dates, a footprint from the hospital card, matching pieces for siblings or for a parent and child.

Take your time. There is no rush on these. If you're not ready for a long session, we start small and add to it later.

Questions about memorial tattoos

How much does a memorial tattoo cost?

Names, dates and handwriting start from [£___]. A memorial portrait starts from [£___]. I'll quote before you pay anything.

Can you copy my dad's handwriting from a card?

Yes. Photograph it flat in daylight, or bring the card in. I trace it exactly, shaky bits included, and we agree the size before the stencil goes on.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes, one person. These sessions can be emotional and I'd rather you had company.
