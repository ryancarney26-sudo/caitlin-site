How it works

Ring first. A quick call to the studio on 01772 378565 saves a wasted trip. If I have a gap, come down with your ID.

What counts as small. Up to about palm size, in simple linework or light shading: a name, a symbol, a small animal, a date, a tiny flower, matching pieces for friends.

Flash. Ready-drawn designs you can pick on the day. [Caitlin: where people can see your flash, such as a highlight on Instagram or a sheet at the studio.] Pick one, pick a spot, and you're usually done within the hour.

Your own small idea. Send it a day or two ahead on Instagram and I'll have it drawn up for when you arrive.

Bring. Photo ID, because you must be 18 and the studio checks. Eat beforehand. Wear something that gives easy access to the spot.
