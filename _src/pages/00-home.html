About me

I'm Caitlin Carney, a tattoo artist at True Colours in Preston. I've always loved drawing portraits more than anything, especially characters from films and TV shows, and that carries straight into my tattooing. And I love anything Christian: faith is a big part of my life.

Most of my clients come from Preston and around Lancashire, and a good few travel from across the North West. You'll find me at the studio on Guildhall Street, next to the Guild Hall in the city centre, Monday to Saturday.

Before 2026 I tattooed as Caitlin O'Brien, so you may have seen my work under that name.

Booking

  1. Send me the idea. A few lines, where on the body, rough size, and any reference pictures. Instagram messages or the studio's form both work.
  2. I come back with a quote. Usually within a few days. Bigger pieces are priced by the session.
  3. A £40 deposit holds your slot. It comes off the final price.
  4. We tattoo. I draw the design up before the day, and we make any changes together before the stencil goes on.

Questions people ask

How much does a tattoo cost?

Small pieces start from £70. Larger custom work is priced by the session: £210 for a half day and £400 for a full day. The prices page has the detail. Send me the idea and I'll give you a clear price before you pay anything.

Do you do colour?

Yes. I work in black and grey and in colour. Tell me what you're picturing and I'll say which suits it.

Do you do cover-ups?

Sometimes, when the old tattoo suits it. Send a clear photo of it in daylight and tell me what you'd like instead, and I'll tell you straight whether I can work with it. Cover-ups start from £140.

Do you do memorial tattoos?

Yes. A portrait of someone you've lost, their handwriting from a card or a note, a name and a date, or a cross that carries them. The portraits and script pages cover how I work on them.

Can I just walk in?

On Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays, yes, for small pieces. Ring the studio first on 01772 378565 to check I have a gap that day. Custom work is always booked in advance.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, £40. It holds your appointment and comes off the final price. It's non-refundable if you cancel with less than a week's notice.

How old do I need to be?

Eighteen, with photo ID on the day. That's the law in the UK, with no exceptions for parental consent.

How do I look after a new tattoo?

I'll go through aftercare with you at the end of your session and send you home with written instructions. In short: keep it clean, keep it moisturised with a thin layer, and keep it out of the sun and out of baths and pools for two weeks.

Can I bring a friend?

One friend is fine. The studio is a working space, so big groups aren't.
