How booking works

  1. Send me the idea. A few lines, where on the body, rough size, and any reference pictures. Instagram messages or the studio's form both work.
  2. I come back with a quote. Usually within a few days. Bigger pieces are priced by the session.
  3. A £40 deposit holds your slot. It comes off the final price.
  4. We tattoo. I draw the design up before the day, and we make any changes together before the stencil goes on.

Before your appointment

  • Eat a proper meal an hour or two before, and bring a drink and a snack for longer sessions.
  • Sleep well the night before, and skip alcohol: it thins the blood and makes the tattoo harder to do.
  • No sunburn on the area. I can't tattoo over it.
  • Wear loose clothes that give easy access to the spot, and that you don't mind getting a little ink on.
  • Bring photo ID. You must be 18, with no exceptions.
  • Bring your reference pictures, or the card with the handwriting, even if you've already sent them.
